TLXO Creative Services is the working name for what I do: building digital products that
work for the people who usually get designed around rather than designed for.

In practice that means accessibility consulting, front-end development, and the sort of
early-stage design review that stops expensive problems from being built in the first
place.

I write here about that work, and separately about things I do for fun. The two are tagged
differently, so you can read one and skip the other.

Get in touch at [hello@tlxo.fi](mailto:hello@tlxo.fi).
